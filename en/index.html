How I work

  • I start from who's reading

    People who find you on Google and people who follow you on Instagram don't want the same things. First I work out who they are, then I write.

  • We look at the numbers every month

    I tell you what worked and what didn't. If a format doesn't perform, we drop it.

  • I only write true things

    No made-up numbers and no promises you can't keep: every figure has a source.

  • The work stays yours

    I write down how the site is built and why. If you ever change supplier, you lose nothing.

About me

I live in Cento, in Emilia-Romagna, Italy. At 17 I spent six months in Sweden, at 19 a year in Australia, on my own, working. Since then, whenever I can, I set off with a backpack to discover new places and people.

After a degree in Gastronomic Sciences I spent years in hospitality and events: a catering company in Modena, hospitality for the Hyundai team in the World Rally Championship. That's where I learned to understand what someone needs before they ask.

In 2024 I started a master's in UX/UI design and changed careers. Today I work for myself: whoever writes to me always talks to me.

When I'm not working I'm in the mountains: climbing, trekking and via ferratas.
