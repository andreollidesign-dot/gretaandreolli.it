Villa Anna

The client

Villa Anna is two separate houses in Lipari, Zagara and Olivetta, 400 metres above the sea with a view of the archipelago. They sleep 4 to 10 guests, with a garden and terraces.

What I did

The brand came first: an orange blossom (zagara) as the symbol, like the larger house and the citrus trees in the garden, then the colours and typefaces. Then the website, designed and coded from scratch: the two houses, the gallery, a guide to Lipari, guest reviews and the booking page.

The site is in Italian, English and German.
